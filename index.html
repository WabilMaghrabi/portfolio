About · 01

From the dental chair to the dashboard

I spent seven years training as a dentist before deciding my real interest was in the patterns behind the data, not just the clinical work itself. I retrained through a structured diploma and Google's Data Analytics Professional Certificate, then started building end-to-end Power BI projects to prove the skills out on real datasets.

My work covers the full pipeline: cleaning messy source data in Power Query, modeling relationships, writing custom DAX measures, and shipping dashboards that hold up under executive review. I've applied this across retail sales worth $24.9M+, HR records for 1,470 employees, and more than 5,000 banking accounts.
